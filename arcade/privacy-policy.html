Privacy Policy for Vanguard X

Effective Date: November 2026
Developer: Needful Thingz Store L.L.C. (Georgia, USA)
Application Package: com.needfulthingz.vanguardx

1. Information We Collect

Vanguard X collects minimal account data strictly required to save your game progress, match credits, unlocked equipment, and multiplayer matchmaking state:

  • Account Credentials: Email address and encrypted user ID (managed via Supabase Authentication) when you choose to register an Operator account.
  • Gameplay Profile: Operator Callsign (username), match scores, virtual Armory credits, and unlocked item flags.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used exclusively to authenticate your Operator profile, sync progress across sessions, and enable real-time multiplayer modes. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security & Deletion

All network traffic between Vanguard X and our cloud backend is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Players may request permanent deletion of their account and associated progression data at any time by contacting support@needfulthingz.com.
