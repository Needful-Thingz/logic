GFCI Troubleshooting

Primary Indicator: Unplug ALL devices on this circuit. Does the GFCI reset now?

Flickering Lights

Primary Indicator: Do the lights get brighter/dimmer when a heavy load (AC/Dryer) turns on?

Dead Short (Instant Trip)

Primary Indicator: Did this happen immediately after plugging something in or turning on a switch?

Circuit Overload

Primary Indicator: Are there multiple high-draw devices (Heater, Microwave, Hairdryer) on this circuit?

Burning Smell / Arcing

Primary Indicator: Is the face of the outlet warm or discolored (brown/black)?

Lost Phase

Primary Indicator: Do 240V appliances (Stove/Dryer) work, but half the 120V lights are off?

Buzzing Panel

Primary Indicator: Can you isolate the sound to a specific breaker?

AFCI Nuisance Trip

Primary Indicator: Does it trip immediately when a specific device (Vacuum/Treadmill) starts?

Appliance Shock

Primary Indicator: Does the shock happen when touching the metal casing of the appliance?

Dead Outlet (No Trip)

Primary Indicator: Is this outlet switched (controlled by a wall switch)?
