Foundation Check

Primary Indicator: Do you see stair-step cracks in the exterior brick or drywall?

Roof Leak Logic

Primary Indicator: Is the leak appearing near a chimney, vent, or valley?

Drywall Diagnostics

Primary Indicator: Is the crack a straight vertical line tape joint?

Flooring Logic

Primary Indicator: Is the hardwood cupping (edges higher than center)?

Windows & Doors

Primary Indicator: Does the door rub against the top of the jamb?

Water Damage

Primary Indicator: Is the water stain active (wet to touch) or dry?

Deck Safety

Primary Indicator: Is the ledger board (attached to house) missing lag bolts?

Insulation & Energy

Primary Indicator: Are there cold spots on walls or ceilings?

Structural Framing

Primary Indicator: Are you removing a wall running perpendicular to joists?

Code & Permits

Primary Indicator: Does the project involve moving plumbing or electric?
