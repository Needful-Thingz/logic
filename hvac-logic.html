AC Blowing Warm

Primary Indicator: Is the outdoor unit fan spinning?

Fan Not Blowing

Primary Indicator: Set thermostat fan to "ON". Does it blow?

Unit Frozen

Primary Indicator: Is the air filter dirty?

Water Leak

Primary Indicator: Is the primary drain line dripping outside?

Loud Noise

Primary Indicator: Is it a metallic screeching sound?

Furnace No Heat

Primary Indicator: Does the small inducer fan start up?

Short Cycling

Primary Indicator: Does it run for less than 2 minutes?

Thermostat Blank

Primary Indicator: Check the float switch at the indoor unit. Is it full of water?

Burning Smell

Primary Indicator: Is it the first time turning on heat this season?

High Bills

Primary Indicator: Is the "Emergency Heat" light on thermostat?
