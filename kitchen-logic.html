Disposal Jammed

Primary Indicator: Is the unit humming but not spinning?

Dishwasher Standing Water

Primary Indicator: Did you recently install a new garbage disposal?

Fridge Warm

Primary Indicator: Is there frost buildup on the back wall of the freezer?

Ice Maker

Primary Indicator: Is the wire "bail arm" in the up position?

Oven Not Heating

Primary Indicator: (Gas Oven) Does the igniter glow orange but no flame appears?

Stove Burner

Primary Indicator: Does wiggling the element make it spark or work intermittently?

Faucet Drip

Primary Indicator: Does it drip from the spout or the handle?

Under Sink Leak

Primary Indicator: Is water leaking from where the basket strainer connects to the sink?

Microwave Dead

Primary Indicator: Did it stop while running and now has no display?

Sink Smell

Primary Indicator: Is there a black sludge visible in the drain opening?
