No Hot Water

Primary Indicator: Is it an Electric Water Heater?

Running Toilet

Primary Indicator: Open the tank. Is the water level going over the overflow tube?

Clogged Toilet

Primary Indicator: Did plunging it fail to clear the stop?

Low Pressure

Primary Indicator: Is the pressure low at every single faucet?

Main Line Backup

Primary Indicator: When you flush the toilet, does water come up in the shower/tub?

Burst Pipe

Primary Indicator: Can you isolate the shut-off valve for that fixture?

Shower Temp Issues

Primary Indicator: Does the water get cold only when someone flushes?

Leaking Hose Bib

Primary Indicator: Is it leaking from the vacuum breaker (cap on top)?

Sump Pump

Primary Indicator: Lift the float switch manually. Does the pump turn on?

Water Hammer

Primary Indicator: Does it bang when the washing machine stops?
