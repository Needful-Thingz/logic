WiFi Dropouts

Primary Indicator: Is the router placed inside a metal cabinet or behind a TV?

Camera Offline

Primary Indicator: Is the camera plugged into power (not battery)?

Doorbell Power

Primary Indicator: Is the doorbell chiming "phantom" rings or buzzing?

Smart Lock Jammed

Primary Indicator: Does the lock beep/grind when trying to lock?

Thermostat Power

Primary Indicator: Does the thermostat verify a "C-Wire" is connected?

Voice Assistant

Primary Indicator: Is the mute button (red ring/light) on?

Smart Bulb

Primary Indicator: Did someone turn off the physical wall switch?

Sensor False Alarm

Primary Indicator: Is the sensor (Motion) facing a window or HVAC vent?

Hub Offline

Primary Indicator: Is the Hub LED flashing blue or red?

Smart Garage

Primary Indicator: Does the app say "Close Error" or "Obstruction"?
