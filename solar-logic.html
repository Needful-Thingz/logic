Inverter Dead

Primary Indicator: Is the AC Disconnect (Lever on wall) in the ON position?

Low Production

Primary Indicator: Is there shading from a new tree or obstacle?

Not Exporting

Primary Indicator: Is the Utility Meter showing an error or blank?

Battery Issue

Primary Indicator: Is the battery switch/breaker in the ON position?

Microinverter Fail

Primary Indicator: Is only ONE specific panel showing 0 production?

Ground Fault (ISO)

Primary Indicator: Did it rain recently?

App Offline

Primary Indicator: Did you change your home WiFi password recently?

Dirty Panels

Primary Indicator: Is there hard water or cement dust on them?

Roof Damage

Primary Indicator: Are wires hanging down touching the roof?

Arc Fault (AFCI)

Primary Indicator: Does the error return immediately after reset?
